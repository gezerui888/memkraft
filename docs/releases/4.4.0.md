## MemKraft 4.4.0

Adds a rule-based write gate for chat-sourced auto extraction. No migration required.

- `extract()` calls whose source starts with `hermes:` (per-turn Hermes sync) no
  longer create template-only entity pages, re-touch pages that are still
  unenriched templates, append facts to such pages, or add bare numbers to
  `fact-registry.md`. The rules are deterministic; no model is called.
- Rejected items are appended to `.memkraft/write-gate.jsonl` with
  `state: proposed` (rotated at 5 MB), so nothing is silently lost.
- `MEMKRAFT_WRITE_GATE=enforce|preview|off` (default `enforce`). `preview`
  writes as before and only logs; `off` disables the gate. Explicit sources
  (`memkraft extract`, `track`, `update`) are unchanged.
- `memkraft gate-archive` counts template-only entity pages; `--apply` moves
  them to `.memkraft/archive/write-gate/<batch>/` with a manifest, and
  `--restore <batch>` puts them back. Files are moved, never deleted.

### Verification

- `tests/test_write_gate.py` (7 cases) and the full suite pass.
- On a copy of a live 14.7k-file Hermes store, 11,567 of 11,570 entity pages
  were template-only. After `gate-archive --apply`: cold first search
  1.17 s → 0.55 s; 20 recall queries 2.56 s → 0.63 s; 17/20 queries returned
  identical top-5 results and none of the 20 had an entity page in its top-5
  before the archive.
