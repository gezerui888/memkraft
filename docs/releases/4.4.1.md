## MemKraft 4.4.1

Hermes recall skips injected lessons below a relevance floor. No migration required.

- Per-turn Hermes prefetch passes `min_score` (default 0.03) to
  `development_inject_for_task()`. `MEMKRAFT_HERMES_LESSON_MIN_SCORE` overrides
  it; `0` injects every recalled lesson as before.
- The library default of `development_inject_for_task()` is unchanged (0.0).

### Verification

- `tests/test_lesson_min_score.py` and the full suite pass.
- On a copy of a live Hermes store, 25 recall queries injected lessons for 7.
  Five of those matched only through a quoted reply prefix (score below 0.03)
  and were unrelated to the query. With the floor, 2 relevant blocks remain and
  injected text drops from 7,545 to 1,381 characters.
